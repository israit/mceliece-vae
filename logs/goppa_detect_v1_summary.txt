  Interpretation (honest):
   - Positive control: the same VAE that reconstructs correct keys at chance level does learn keys from weakened generators
     (up to 97.3% bit accuracy), so its failure on correct keys reflects the randomness of the keys, not an under-powered model.
   - The VAE flags generators whose keys have biased bits, including keys built from VAE-generated S (the original thesis idea).
   - But the VAE adds nothing over the simplest test: its bit accuracy equals the majority-bit predictor in every family except
     the two with repeated keys; it never reconstructed a key that was not in its training set; it recovered only 2% of the

   - It does not see a weak g when L, S and P are random (and VAE g sampled bit by bit is close to uniform anyway).
