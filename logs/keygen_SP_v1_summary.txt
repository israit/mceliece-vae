Note: 'CSPRNG' in the script output = NumPy default_rng (PCG64), a uniform pseudo-random generator, NOT a cryptographically secure one. In the paper it is called 'uniform RNG baseline'.

Interpretation:
  - Functional: VAE-generated S and P give working McEliece keys after a validity filter (S) / projection (P).
  - Not faster: 2.3x (S, z=16) to 22x (S, z=64) and ~700x (P) slower than a CSPRNG.
  - Not random enough: z=16 S collapses to a few matrices (min-entropy 0.30 bits; bits almost constant, bias 0.494 vs 0.013);
    P never valid without projection and its positions are strongly non-uniform (chi2 p = 0 vs 0.999 for CSPRNG).
  - Key-generation cost is dominated by the Goppa polynomial and linear algebra; S/P generation is a small, avoidable part.
